# Suno ending music creation pack

> Superseded for new generations by [the v6 specification](suno-v6-ending-music.json) and the [v6 browser creation record](suno-v6-create-record.md). This document preserves the earlier v5.5-era prompts for reference.

Create these as **original instrumentals** in Suno Custom mode. Do not upload reference audio and do not enter lyrics. Use the account's visible model, duration setting, and credits at creation time.

## Defeat loop — `Fallen Standard`

**Style prompt**

> Stylized low-poly presidential-adventure game defeat loop, 76 BPM in D minor. Sparse felt piano, muted low brass, distant military snare, detuned music-box fragments, and a soft analog pulse. Begin with a two-bar sinking introduction, then an eight-bar lament, then a restrained sixteen-bar loop that returns clearly to beat one with a short controlled tail. Dialogue- and UI-safe, emotionally defeated but not horror. Leave spectral room for gameplay sound effects. Instrumental only, no vocal lead.

**Exclude**

> vocals, chanting, recognizable melodies, jump scares, huge trailer hits, harsh distortion, long reverb tail, abrupt ending, trap hi-hats, crowd noise

Set Weirdness around 35 and Style Influence medium to strong if those controls are available. For a second usable take, keep every setting fixed and replace the felt-piano lead with low cello.

## Victory loop — `Golden Signature`

**Style prompt**

> Stylized low-poly presidential-adventure game victory loop, 108 BPM in G major. Bright pizzicato strings, warm ceremonial brass fanfare, handclaps and light toms, celesta, gentle snare, and glockenspiel. Open with a two-bar logo stinger, then a sixteen-bar celebratory hook that returns cleanly to beat one. Confident, playful, and heroic without becoming bombastic; dialogue- and UI-safe with room for gameplay effects. Instrumental only, no vocal lead.

**Exclude**

> vocals, chanting, recognizable melodies, huge trailer hits, harsh distortion, long reverb tail, abrupt ending, trap hi-hats, crowd noise

Set Weirdness around 30 and Style Influence medium to strong if those controls are available. For a second usable take, keep every setting fixed and change only the lead from glockenspiel to bright trumpet.

## Acceptance checks

- The musical return to the first beat must be clear enough to loop without an audible seam.
- Reject any result with sung words, an extended fade, a hard cutoff, or a melody that resembles existing music.
- The two tracks must remain distinct: loss is restrained and minor; victory is bright and ceremonial.
- After download, audition each result in a short repeating loop before assigning it to the defeat or victory screen.
