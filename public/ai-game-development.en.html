AI-assisted game development · WebGL · action adventure

An AI-assisted 3D adventure—not just a prompt demo

The Legend of Trump · A White House Adventure is a single-player 3D action-adventure that runs directly in the browser. Explore the White House grounds, collect eight emeralds, find a sword, shield and bow, charge a heavy punch, then face the Iron Commander.

React 19, Three.js, React Three Fiber, TypeScript and Vite

What “AI-assisted game” means here

AI supported creative exploration, prototyping and development iteration. The published result is a playable, curated software project—not a page that merely displays a generated clip. It does not call a chat model or generate a world while you play, so no account or remote generative-AI API is required.

This is a concrete example for people exploring AI-made games, AI game development, 3D browser games and WebGL games: the work lands in controls, combat, environments and a finishable game loop.

Verifiable combat facts

  • Unarmed combat has a left jab, right cross and front kick; hold attack to charge a heavy punch.
  • After full charge, continued holding slowly drains stamina; release delivers a stronger hit with knockback and hit-stop.
  • Holding guard drains stamina; every successful block spends one fixed 40-point stamina section.
  • Blocking with fewer than 40 stamina causes a guard break: stamina is emptied and the player loses half a heart.
